\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Joint integration.}
Harmony~\citep{korsunsky2019harmony}, Scanorama~\citep{hie2019scanorama} and mutual-nearest-neighbour
correction~\citep{haghverdi2018mnn} produce a new representation of all inputs, as do the spatially aware
GraphST~\citep{long2023graphst} and STAligner~\citep{zhou2023staligner}, which train graph encoders on all sections
jointly, so analyses built on the previous reference must be redone.

\paragraph{Mapping into a reference.}
Symphony~\citep{kang2021symphony} and scArches~\citep{lotfollahi2022scarches} map queries without changing the
reference, but the reference must be built in their own model rather than in an embedding the analyst already uses.
The lightest reference-preserving alternatives are affine or linear post-hoc adapters fitted after the fact: a
translation (mean centring), a second-order affine map (CORAL~\citep{sun2016coral}) or a linear map fitted on matched
cells; we include all three as baselines.

\paragraph{Optimal transport.}
SCOT~\citep{demetci2022scot} aligns modalities with Gromov--Wasserstein transport over $k$-NN geodesics. USHER~\citep{pratapa2025usher} couples
FGW correspondences with a low-capacity transform in an EM loop to move foundation-model embeddings into a fixed
target. \frost inherits this design and changes how and when the structure is computed.

\paragraph{Partial overlap.}
Both USHER and \frost solve balanced FGW, so all source mass is transported, although sections from different platforms
need not cover the same tissue. RAFT-UP~\citep{wu2026raftup} aligns partially overlapping slices with a fused supervised
Gromov--Wasserstein formulation that adds a pairwise distance-consistency cutoff and relaxed marginals that leave
unmatched mass untransported; scArches~\citep{lotfollahi2022scarches} maps queries that contain cell types absent from
the reference. Neither is evaluated here.

\paragraph{Other mapping, alignment and transport methods.}
SpCAST~\citep{spcast} integrates dissociated single-cell RNA-seq with single-cell-resolved
spatial transcriptomics, with an emphasis on scalability and interpretability. INST-Align~\citep{han2026instalign} couples a coordinate-based
deformation network with a canonical expression field, an implicit neural representation mapping spatial coordinates
to expression embeddings, for joint alignment and reconstruction. STG3Net~\citep{fang2024stg3net} and
SemanticST~\citep{zahedi2025semanticst} learn graph-based representations, for multi-slice domain identification
with batch correction and for clustering and integration respectively; like GraphST, they produce new embeddings,
which is a mismatch to the problem setting here rather than a shortcoming of the methods. LCN-Sinkhorn~\citep{gasteiger2021lcn}
and Geodesic Sinkhorn~\citep{huguet2022geodesic} make entropic transport log-linear, the latter by diffusing a heat
kernel on a manifold graph. We keep exact geodesics instead: caching makes the number of structure computations
$2B$, independent of $T$, so building them is no longer the cost that such approximations address.
