\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Problem setting}\label{sec:setting}
Let $\mathbf{X}^{t}\in\mathbb{R}^{n_t\times d}$ be foundation-model (or PCA) embeddings of cells profiled on a
\emph{reference} platform, together with their cell-type annotations, and let
$\mathbf{X}^{s}\in\mathbb{R}^{n_s\times d}$ be embeddings of cells from the same tissue profiled on a different
\emph{source} platform. We seek a map $f_\theta:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R}^{d}$ that places source cells in the
reference coordinate system, under one hard constraint: $\mathbf{X}^{t}$ is never modified. Every downstream object
built on the reference---a nearest-neighbour index, a classifier, an annotated atlas---therefore remains valid
after integration, and reference drift is zero by construction rather than by tuning. Source annotations are
never used for fitting; they serve only for evaluation. Spatial coordinates are used only within each section, to
describe a cell's microenvironment (Section~\ref{sec:frost}); no coordinate is ever compared across sections.

\subsection{Base algorithm: USHER}\label{sec:usher}
\frost is built on USHER~\citep{pratapa2025usher}, which alternates between estimating cross-domain
correspondences and fitting a low-capacity transform. In the E-step, a soft coupling $\mathbf{P}$ between the
transformed source $f_\theta(\mathbf{X}^{s})$ and the reference is obtained from the fused Gromov--Wasserstein
(FGW) problem~\citep{vayer2019fgw}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fgw}
  \mathbf{P}^{\star}=\arg\min_{\mathbf{P}\in\Pi}\;(1-\alpha)\,\langle \mathbf{M},\mathbf{P}\rangle
  +\alpha\sum_{i,k,j,\ell}\bigl(C^{s}_{ik}-C^{t}_{j\ell}\bigr)^{2}P_{ij}P_{k\ell},
\end{equation}
solved with entropic regularisation $\varepsilon$~\citep{cuturi2013sinkhorn}. The cross-domain cost combines a
feature term and an auxiliary term,
$\mathbf{M}=\gamma\,\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{aux}}+(1-\gamma)\,\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{feat}}$, with
$M_{\mathrm{feat}}(i,j)=d\bigl(f_\theta(\mathbf{x}^{s}_{i}),\mathbf{x}^{t}_{j}\bigr)$; the first iteration uses
$\gamma=1$ as a warm start. $\Cs$ and $\Ct$ are within-domain geodesic distances on $k$-nearest-neighbour graphs
of the embeddings, following SCOT~\citep{demetci2022scot}. Rows of $\mathbf{P}^{\star}$ with high entropy or
low maximum are discarded (entropic filtering), and a linear assignment~\citep{kuhn1955hungarian} on the remainder
yields one-to-one matches $\pi(i)$. In the M-step, $f_\theta$ is updated with Adam on
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mstep}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta)=\lambda_{\mathrm{cross}}\,\mathbb{E}_i\bigl\|f_\theta(\mathbf{x}^{s}_{i})-\mathbf{x}^{t}_{\pi(i)}\bigr\|
  +\lambda_{\mathrm{var}}\bigl\|\mathrm{Var}\,f_\theta(\mathbf{X}^{s})-\mathrm{Var}\,\mathbf{X}^{t}\bigr\|_{1}
  +\lambda_{\mathrm{id}}\,\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{id}}(\theta),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{id}}$ penalises departure from the identity map. Embeddings are standardised with
reference statistics before the E- and M-steps and de-standardised afterwards.

\subsection{FROST}\label{sec:frost}
\frost keeps the EM skeleton of USHER and changes five components: (i)--(v) below.
Table~\ref{tab:usher_vs_frost} lists every difference from the USHER reference implementation as it was run in
this study, together with the two intermediate variants used in the ablations; a numerical comparison with USHER
is given in Section~\ref{sec:res_usher}.

\paragraph{(i) Auxiliary cost from the reference labels.}
For each source cell we first align first moments (subtracting the source mean and adding the reference mean),
then take the label frequencies $\mathbf{p}^{s}_{i}$ among its 15 nearest reference cells. Reference cells carry
one-hot vectors $\mathbf{e}_{y_j}$ of their annotations, and
$M_{\mathrm{aux}}(i,j)=\|\mathbf{p}^{s}_{i}-\mathbf{e}_{y_j}\|_{2}$, divided by its maximum. No label model is
trained on source gene expression, so the auxiliary signal depends only on the embeddings and the reference
annotation.

\paragraph{(ii) Structure matrices built once, on frozen inputs.}
For every cell we sum the counts of its 15 nearest spatial neighbours within its own section, over the genes shared
by the pair, and reduce them to 50 principal components, $\mathbf{N}_i$. Structure is computed on the augmented
features $[\mathbf{x}_i,\;s\,\mathbf{N}_i]$, with $s=\overline{\|\mathbf{x}^{t}\|}/\overline{\|\mathbf{N}^{t}\|}$
so that the two blocks have comparable norms. $\Cs$ and $\Ct$ are exact hop-count geodesics on the symmetrised
$k$-NN graphs ($k=30$, cosine, unit edge weights) of these features, after projection onto their top 64 principal
axes when the dimension exceeds 64. Each is normalised by its maximum. Both are computed from the \emph{unmapped}
source and from the reference, once per batch, and cached across EM iterations. With $B$ batches and $T$
iterations, \frost therefore builds $2B$ structure matrices, whereas USHER recomputes both in every batch and
iteration ($2BT$). Freezing $\Cs$ is a departure from USHER, in which $\Cs$ is defined on the current estimate
$f_\theta(\mathbf{X}^{s})$. In \frost FGW compares the source's own neighbourhood structure with the reference's,
and the cost of building it is paid once. The microenvironment block adds no measurable cost
(Section~\ref{sec:res_cost}), and its contribution to accuracy cannot be separated from that of freezing
(Section~\ref{sec:res_niche}). The physical radius spanned by 15 neighbours differs between platforms (median
16.7--33.9\um).

\paragraph{(iii) Reference sampling with a per-class floor.}
FGW is solved on a reference subset of 20{,}000 cells. Its class composition follows the source composition
estimated from the auxiliary labels of (i), but every reference class receives at least $\min(200,n_c)$ cells,
where $n_c$ is the number of available cells; if the total exceeds the budget, only the allocation above the floor
is scaled down. Because balanced FGW must transport all source mass, a purely proportional subset leaves too few
reference cells of rare types for their source counterparts to be matched to. The floor was selected on a
validation split (Section~\ref{sec:res_floor}).

\paragraph{(iv) Linear M-step with identity regularisation.}
$f_\theta(\mathbf{x})=\mathbf{W}\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{b}$ in standardised coordinates, with
$\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{id}}=\mathbb{E}_i\|(\mathbf{W}-\mathbf{I})\,\mathbf{x}^{s}_{i}\|^{2}/d$ and
$\lambda_{\mathrm{id}}=0.3$. The bias is excluded from the penalty so that global translation remains free.

\paragraph{(v) Post-hoc second-moment matching.}
After EM, the mapped source embeddings $\mathbf{Z}=f_\theta(\mathbf{X}^{s})$ are passed through a closed-form
CORAL transform~\citep{sun2016coral},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coral}
  \tilde{\mathbf{z}}=(\mathbf{z}-\hat{\bm{\mu}}_{s})\,\hat{\bm{\Sigma}}_{s}^{-1/2}\bm{\Sigma}_{t}^{1/2}+\bm{\mu}_{t},
\end{equation}
with source statistics estimated on training-tile cells, reference statistics on all reference cells, and a ridge of
$0.01\,\mathrm{tr}(\bm{\Sigma})/d$ added to both covariances. The step uses no labels, takes seconds on a CPU and
does not touch the reference. It buys distribution-level agreement at a measurable cost in local density fidelity;
we quantify this trade-off in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\subsection{Variants used in the ablations}\label{sec:variants}
\textbf{USHER-ST} shares (i), (iii) and (iv) with \frost but builds structure as in our earlier pipeline. $\Ct$ is
the exact geodesic on the reference embeddings alone, cached. $\Cs$ is recomputed in every iteration on
$f_\theta(\mathbf{X}^{s})$, using $L=512$ landmarks chosen as the candidates closest to the centroids of a $k$-means
fit on $10L$ randomly drawn cells. With $D_{il}$ the hop distance from cell $i$ to landmark $l$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:landmark}
  C^{s}_{ik}=-\frac{1}{\beta}\log\sum_{l=1}^{L}\exp\bigl(-\beta\,(D_{il}+D_{kl})\bigr),\qquad \beta=2,
\end{equation}
a soft minimum over paths routed through a landmark, followed by zeroing the diagonal and normalising by the
maximum. \textbf{USHER-CC} is USHER-ST followed by (v). \textbf{\frost w/o CORAL} omits (v). A
microenvironment-only variant, which builds structure on $\mathbf{N}$ alone, was run with a single seed.

\paragraph{Hyperparameters.}
All 12 pairs use one configuration: $\alpha=0.3$, $\gamma=0.4$ ($\gamma=1$ at the first iteration),
$\varepsilon=0.1$, $k=30$, cosine $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{feat}}$; entropic filtering removes rows above the 90th
percentile of row entropy or below the 10th percentile of row maximum; sketches of 4{,}300 cells;
10 EM iterations of 100 Adam steps each (learning rate $10^{-3}$); $\lambda_{\mathrm{cross}}=0.9$,
$\lambda_{\mathrm{var}}=0.1$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{id}}=0.3$. $\alpha$ and $\gamma$ are the values recommended
in~\citep{pratapa2025usher}. FGW and the M-step run in single precision.
