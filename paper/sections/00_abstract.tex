\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Motivation:} Cell embeddings from different spatial transcriptomics platforms disagree. Most
integration methods, including spatially aware ones, re-embed all data, moving the reference that downstream analyses
rest on. We align platforms without modifying the reference.

\noindent\textbf{Results:} \frost maps source cells into a fixed reference with fused Gromov--Wasserstein transport.
Building on USHER, it computes structure matrices once per batch rather than per EM iteration, fits a linear
identity-regularised map and applies closed-form moment matching. On 12 SPATCH pairs it ranks among the top three of
11 methods on seven cell-level metrics and transfers labels better than spatially aware GraphST, with zero reference
drift. It needs 25-fold fewer structure computations and about 7.5-fold less EM time than USHER at USHER's 25 EM
iterations. Imputation from reference queries and deconvolution favour unaligned or locally corrected embeddings,
bounding its scope.
