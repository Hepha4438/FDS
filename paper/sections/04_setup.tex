\section{Experimental setup}\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data.}\label{sec:data}
We use SPATCH~\citep{ren2025spatch}: colon adenocarcinoma (COAD), ovarian cancer (OV) and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC),
one patient and one section per platform. Xenium 5K is the reference; CosMx 6K, Visium HD FFPE, Visium HD FF and
Stereo-seq are the sources, giving 12 pairs. The Visium HD FF data come from the SPATCH portal and are
described in the preprint~\citep{ren2024spatchpreprint} but not in the published benchmark; the preprint notes
possible RNA degradation in those samples. Pairs within a tissue share the patient and the reference, so they are
not independent. Each assay keeps about 40{,}000--45{,}000 high-quality cells (label-stratified subsample). A 500\um
checkerboard splits every section into training tiles, used for fitting, and held-out tiles, used for evaluation.
Labels comprise 16 shared groups, with six coarse groups for pairs that recover fewer than three. HCC Stereo-seq
recovers only hepatocytes, so label metrics are not interpreted for that pair.

\paragraph{Representations.}\label{sec:reps}
Each pair uses PCA of log-normalised expression or scGPT-spatial~\citep{wang2025scgptspatial} (Supplementary
Table~\ref{S-tab:reps}), chosen once per pair before alignment by within-assay label recoverability on held-out tiles.
This choice uses held-out tiles, but every reference-preserving method, \frost included, receives the same
representation for a given pair, so it cannot favour \frost within that group. The bias that remains runs the other
way: Harmony and Scanorama are fitted transductively on all source cells, held-out tiles included, whereas \frost is
fitted on the training tiles only. This bias favours those two baselines, so the margins by which \frost exceeds them
should be read as lower bounds.

\paragraph{Baselines.}\label{sec:baselines}
Reference-preserving: no alignment (raw), mean centring (center), CORAL~\citep{sun2016coral}, source-side MNN
(MNN-ref)~\citep{haghverdi2018mnn}, and a label-informed ridge regression that pairs source and reference cells by
the auxiliary labels of \frost and applies the same moment matching (ridge). Centring, CORAL and ridge form the
family of affine and linear post-hoc adapters, the lightweight alternative to iterative alignment; MNN-ref applies
local, cell-specific translations. Reference-modifying:
Harmony~\citep{korsunsky2019harmony} and Scanorama~\citep{hie2019scanorama}, both fitted transductively as noted above (with its default matching threshold Scanorama returned the input unchanged on all
three Stereo-seq pairs and on HCC Visium HD FFPE; we report the default), and Symphony~\citep{kang2021symphony}, which builds its own count-based space.
Spatially aware: GraphST~\citep{long2023graphst}, a representative spatial-integration method, at published
defaults on a common subsample (Section~\ref{sec:res_spatial}).

\paragraph{Metrics.}\label{sec:metrics}
The main benchmark uses \texttt{scib-metrics}~\citep{luecken2022scib} group scores for batch mixing (batch\_v2) and
biological conservation (bio\_v2), their weighted total (total\_v2), and four task metrics: label transfer over
shared groups (xfer\_main) and over all groups (xfer\_all), purity of rare types (rare\_purity), and prediction of a
cell's microenvironment class from reference neighbours (knn\_xen). Definitions are in Supplementary
Section~S2. GraphST re-embeds both sides, so it is compared on the common subsample through
transfer, mixing and \emph{reference drift}, $1-J$ with $J$ the mean Jaccard overlap of each reference cell's 15
nearest neighbours before and after integration.

\paragraph{Statistics.}\label{sec:stats}
For each comparison we report the median paired difference, the number of pairs $n$ and a two-sided exact Wilcoxon
signed-rank test. Differences with $p<0.05$ are called significant; otherwise we report no significant difference,
which is not evidence of equivalence. With 12 pairs the smallest attainable $p$ is $4.9\times10^{-4}$. Stochastic methods use three seeds; the GraphST comparison uses one.
