attention weights are silently not loaded because parameter names are not remapped; we remapped them and verified

\subsection*{S2. Metric definitions}\label{sec:supp_metrics}
Group scores follow \texttt{scib-metrics} without min--max scaling across methods: batch\_v2 is the mean of iLISI,
kBET, BRAS and graph connectivity; bio\_v2 is the mean of NMI, ARI, label ASW, cLISI and isolated-label score;
total\_v2 $=0.4\,$batch\_v2 $+\,0.6\,$bio\_v2. xfer\_main is the balanced accuracy of a 15-NN vote of reference
labels, after centring on the reference mean and unit normalisation, over shared recoverable groups; xfer\_all is the
same over all groups with at least 20 cells on both sides. rare\_purity is the fraction of same-label reference
neighbours among the 15-NN of cells from groups below 2\% of held-out tiles. knn\_xen is the balanced accuracy of
predicting a cell's microenvironment class (eight $k$-means classes of spatial-neighbour label composition) from its 15
reference neighbours. In the GraphST comparison, mixing is $1-\max(f_{\mathrm{same}}-0.5,0)/0.5$, with
$f_{\mathrm{same}}$ the fraction of same-platform cells among 15 neighbours in the pooled unit-normalised embedding;
transfer uses no centring.

\subsection*{S3. Computing environment}
Kaggle, 2$\times$NVIDIA T4 (16\,GB each), about 29\,GB RAM, 4 vCPUs. Main benchmark: PyTorch 2.10.0+cu128, NumPy
2.0.2, SciPy 1.16.3, AnnData 0.11.4, POT 0.9.6.post1, scib-metrics 0.6.1, tangram-sc 1.0.4. GraphST comparison:
PyTorch 2.11.0+cu128, Scanpy 1.12.4, AnnData 0.11.4. GraphST, harmonypy, Scanorama and symphonypy versions were not
recorded~\todo{versions}. Peak memory was not measured.

\subsection*{S4. Microenvironment term}\label{sec:supp_niche}
The difference between \frost and USHER-CC measures the joint effect of three simultaneous changes to $\Cs$: adding
the microenvironment, replacing landmark distances with exact geodesics, and replacing per-iteration recomputation on
$f_\theta(\mathbf{X}^{s})$ with a single computation on the unmapped source. On the PCA pairs a fourth difference is
added: the 64-dimensional low-rank projection applies to the augmented features but not to the 50-dimensional
embeddings. We cannot separate the contribution of the microenvironment with the data at hand. A microenvironment-only variant (structure on $\mathbf{N}$ alone) was run with a single seed
and is reported only in Table~\ref{tab:imp_rev}. Under the reversed
imputation protocol it recovers part of the gene-level loss on COAD Stereo-seq (Table~\ref{tab:imp_rev}), still
below no alignment.
